INTERNAL QUALITY ASSURANCE CELL
An Internal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC) is a vital component of higher education institutions, established to ensure and
enhance the quality of education and overall institutional performance. It functions as a self-regulatory body, promoting a
culture of continuous improvement and accountability. Quality sustenance and enhancement in education are key
challenges in higher education. To institutionalize the process of continuous improvement, the Internal Quality Assurance
Cell (IQAC) was established at PCTE on 10 th July 2021.
Key Roles and Responsibilities of IQAC Cell:
Quality Enhancement: Improve quality of education, research, and other institutional
activities.
Vision
Self-Assessment: Conducts self-assessments to identify strengths, and areas for
improvement.
To uphold the institute's
standing as a leader in
education through a
dedication to ongoing
development.
Data Analysis: Analyses various data sources, such as student feedback, faculty performance,
etc
Planning and Implementation: Quality enhancement and oversees their implementation.
Monitoring and Evaluation: Quality initiatives and evaluates their effectiveness.
Documentation: Maintains comprehensive documentation of all quality assurance activities.
Mission
Stakeholder Involvement: By involving all stakeholders in the quality enhancement process.

PCTE RESEARCH AND DEVELOPMENT CELL
The Research and Development Cell’s mission is to promote research activities across various disciplines and provide a
supportive environment for faculty and students to engage in creative and impactful research.
Major Activities of the R & D Cell are Focused on:
1. Hosting national and international conferences, seminars, and workshops
2. Encouraging collaboration between different departments and academic disciplines to foster innovative research
solutions and address complex challenges.
3. Assisting faculty and students in preparing and submitting research papers, articles, and book chapters to reputable
journals and publishers, enhancing their visibility in the academic community.
4. Promoting the development and commercialisation of innovative
technologies and research outcomes through partnerships with
industries, stakeholders and with help of E-cell at PCTE.
5. Organising training sessions and capacity-building programs to
enhance research skills, methodologies, and knowledge of the latest
trends in various fields of study.
6. Offering mentorship and support for students in their research
projects, thesis, and dissertation work, guiding them through the
research process and requirements.
7. Identifying and facilitating access to funding sources for research
projects, including internal grants, government initiatives, and industry
partnerships.

NATIONAL SERVICES SCHEME (NSS)
The National Service Scheme (NSS) is a public service program in India, aimed at developing students' personality
through community service. Established in 1969 by the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports, the NSS promotes a
sense of social responsibility, teamwork, and leadership among young people. Volunteers participate in a range of
activities, like Tree plantation, Cleaning campus , Health camps in villages, testing of drinking water in local area.
Woman health awareness program. from health and hygiene awareness to environmental conservation, literacy
drives, and disaster relief. Through these initiatives, the NSS fosters the spirit of national integration and community
development, encouraging students to contribute positively to society.

Public Notice
Advt. No. AICTE/Legal/04(01)/2007
Sub: Instructions to Technical Institutions, Universities including A
Deemed to be Universities imparting Technical Education regarding
matters concerning charging of fees, refund of fees and other student
related issues.
All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) has been empowered
interalia under section 10 (n) of AICTE Act to "take all necessary steps to
prevent commercialization of technical education" In compliance with
the provision under AICTE Act and in the light of directions of Govt. of
India issued under section 20 (1) of AICTE Act vide Letter No. (A) Section),
it has been decided to issue instructions to the Technical institutions,
Universities including Deemed to be Universities imparting Technical
Education in the matters concerning students. Whereas it has come to the
notice of the AICTE that Technical Institutions and Universities including
Deemed to be Universities, are admitting students to technical education
programmes long before the actual starting of an academic session;
collecting full fee from the admitted students; and, retaining their
school/institution's leaving certificates in the original; And Whereas,
Institutions and Universities are also reportedly confiscating the fee paid
if a student fails to join by such dates; And, Whereas, certificates in original
are being detained by institutions and Universities to force retention of
admitted students; 1
And, Whereas the time-limit for students to join the courses/programmes
is also being advanced in some cases unrealistically so as to pre-empt
students/candidates from exercising other options of joining other
institutions of their choice. In the event of a student/candidate
withdrawing before the starting of the course, the wait listed candidate
should be given admissions against the vacant seat. The entire fee
collected from the student, after deduction of the processing fee of not
more than Rs. 1000/- (Rupees one thousand only) shall be refunded and
returned by the Institution/University to the student/candidate
withdrawing from the programme. It would not be permissible for the
Institutions and Universities to retain the School/Institution Leaving
Certificates in original. Should a student leave after joining the course
and if the seat consequently falling vacant has been filled by another
candidate by the last date of admission, the Institution must return the
fee collected with proportionate deductions of monthly fee and
proportionate hostel rent, where applicable. Any violation of instruction
issued by the AICTE, shall call for punitive including withdrawal of
approval and recognition of erring institutions and Universities. AICTE
shall on its own or on receipt of specific complaints from those affected
take all such steps as may be necessary to enforce these directions
