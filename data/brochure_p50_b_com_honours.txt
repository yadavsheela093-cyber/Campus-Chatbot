Bachelor of Commerce in Honors (B. Com Honours)
Duration: 3 Years
Total Seats: 330
Eligibility: 10+2 (any stream)
This three-year, full-time Bachelor of Commerce (Honours) program provides students with a comprehensive foundation in
essential business areas, including Accounting, Management, and Business Statistics, along with critical leadership skills.
Through a curriculum that integrates theoretical insights with practical applications, students gain a deep understanding
of business operations within a global context. Beyond technical expertise, the program fosters critical thinking and
problem-solving abilities, empowering graduates to apply their knowledge effectively in diverse business environments.
