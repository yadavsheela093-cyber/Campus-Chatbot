PCTE Ludhiana official brochure 2026 - BTTM
Bachelor of Travel & Tourism Management (BTTM)
Duration: 4 Years
Total Seats: 60
Eligibility: 10+2 (any stream)
Tourism is the 3rd largest industry which is flourishing over the world and we are glad to be at the core offering it. The four year
undergraduate program helps in learning about different cultures and to built diverse set of skills to work in travel and tourism sector where
you will be taught about Travel Agent Airline Management, Tour Guiding, Hospitality Industry, Marketing, Human Resource Management,
Hotel & Resort Management etc Study combines a well-rounded academic curriculum with hands-on experiential learning.
Beyond the Classroom
Cabin Crew Training: PCTE BTTM Has launched cabin crew
training in the in-house well developed airlines lab.
Passports Making Activity: The travel and tourism students
understand the process of passport making. Every student has
to get at least 20 passports processed during the semester.
Training in Travel Agencies: Students prepare tour packages,
process visa applications of various countries, make air ticketing
reservations (on online portals), act as a tour guide & Tour
Coordinators organized by college every year. The students are
also involved in sales and marketing of tour packages, Event
planning, Railway Ticketing (IRCTC), etc.
Association with AIESEC: The Department of Travel and
Tourism maintains an active collaboration with the International
Association of Students in Economics and Business (AIESEC),
offering students valuable national and international internship
opportunities.
Placements: PCTE students have excelled in placements across
diverse sectors, including top airlines like United Airlines,
Singapore Airlines, Air Asia, and Indigo, as well as prestigious
roles in airport services, event management, and retail. In travel
agencies, they've made their mark with industry leaders like
Make My Trip and Thomas Cook. Our graduates have reached
global heights, securing positions in countries such as the USA,
Canada, the UK, and more. Additionally, PCTEians have
successfully contributed to ministries of tourism in countries like
Bhutan and Lesotho. Apart from this, they have also been placed
in the State Tourism Boards like Punjab Heritage Promotion
Board, Jammu-Kashmir Tourism Development Corporation etc.
Some of our students are exceptionally doing well in their own
businesses.
Notably, one of our students achieved a milestone by securing
the highest package of Rs 50 Lacs at a position of Cabin Crew
during placement with United Airlines, highlighting the
exceptional opportunities and success our graduates attain in
the competitive aviation industry.
Globetrotter: It is a competition held during the Hospitality
Olympics event every year where the students participate in
Geography Quiz. The Quiz is divided into 5 parts: Countries,
Flags, Capitals, Currency and Famous Destinations &
Personalities.
Event Management: Students are opting for successful career
options as an entrepreneur within the event management field.
