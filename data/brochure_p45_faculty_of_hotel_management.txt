PCTE Ludhiana official brochure 2026 - Faculty of Hotel Management
Faculty of Hotel Management
We prepare professionals for dynamic and demanding industries, not only do we focus on practical training but also on overall development and grooming of
personality. The hotel management college at PCTE has robust research and development unit that provides students with exceptional hands on training in
Indian and global culinary innovations.
Over the Years, the faculty of Hotel Management has registered and have set various records such as for Guinness Book of Records & Limca Book of Records by
making 1241 varieties of parathas in 9 minutes & 10 seconds during the 2018-2019 school year.
They also made it to the India Book of World Records by organizing "MOJITO FIESTA" where 110 Students made 110 different types of Virgin Mojitos in 110 seconds.
In 2019, a record in the Limca Book of Records was registered by preparing the world's largest thali of 96 inches in diameter with 270 dishes of all states and union
territories of India.
It goes on as the students displayed 100 varieties of Halwaizzas i.e. Halwa served on a Pizza Base to explore their creativity.
In 2022, yet another Limca Book of Records was registered where the students prepared 114 different varieties of coffee in the Cafiesta Event. Besides this, another
world record was set where 160 students made 300 samosa variations in the Great Samotsav.
In 2023, students prepared the largest Indian traditional style thali, “ATULNIYA BHARAT” that was 2.44 m in diameter and had 270 authentic dishes prepared from
the cuisines of all 28 states and 8 Union Territories of India.
Another event, “Salaam-e-India 2023” witnessed 405 incarnations of Golgappas, a grand tribute to the tapestry of street food culture that thrives in every nook and
cranny of our diverse nation.
In 2024, ‘India’s Akalpaniya Vanjanam”, Pcte students developed 524 variations of Samosas, using millets as base, creating entreprenuership opportunities /
Startups for individual from both rural and urban sectors.
In 2024, PCTE has aimed to promote rural tourism, which involves visiting rural areas, villages, and the countryside to experience local culture, traditions, cuisine,
and landscapes. This initiative has emphasized sustainable development by supporting local communities and has offered travelers authentic and immersive
experiences.
"In 2024, hotel management introduced Jagolates, a healthier alternative to traditional chocolates, made from ingredients like channa, jaggery, and turmeric.
These innovative snacks combine health benefits with convenience, impacting both health-conscious consumers and the food industry.Additionally, hotel
management launched a range of oil-free mayonnaises, including garlic curd, pary pary curd, herb curd, and honey mustard mayo, along with 15 other unique
varieties. These healthier, oil-free condiments are set to redefine the market.”
The faculty and students of Hotel Management research new cuisine every year. Some of the recent ones are:
Tribal Cuisine: A showcase of different Indian tribal dishes prepared in
bare minimum spices and cooked in wood fire, leaves, bamboo,
earthenware as originally used by different tribes of India.
Blue Cuisine: A cuisine of lost & unknown recipes and dishes of Mughals,
Rajputana, Nizams & Nawabs, and dishes from "Royal' India. It was
introduced to revive slow cuisine.
White Cuisine: A healthy cuisine made by using raw, steamed and boiled
food. Students prepared 160 types of dishes using the same method from
all over India.
Green Cuisine: A powerful cuisine introduced to think about food in a
healthier & beneficial way, the faculty and students prepared dishes with
ingredients that not only tasted delicious but also healed diseases.
Pink Cuisine: A cuisine introducing fresh flowers with great health & taste
values.
Curry Cuisine: Fifteen types of various curries with immunity boosting
agents which are known for healing diseases were introduced by the
department.
Stone Cuisine: The Cuisine was being prepared by cooking on the stone,
which has earthy food value.
Slow Fire Cuisine: This Cuisine was served by using slow cooling method,
cooking in bamboo, earthen pots etc to revive the food value & taste.
Global Cuisine: The fusion Cuisine was made by mixing Cuisine of Indian
with Italian, French, English, Chinese & Thai Cuisine.
Ayushman Cuisine: Ayushman Cuisine is inspired by Ayurvedic and Satvic
principles, focusing on wholesome, onion- and garlic-free preparations. It
celebrates signature Indian dishes while emphasizing health benefits
through ingredients renowned for their medicinal properties. With a
mission to promote wellness and prevent diseases.
Annameva Jayeta – A cultural journey through 300 rice-based dishes
from 28 states of India, tracing flavors from the ancient era to today.
