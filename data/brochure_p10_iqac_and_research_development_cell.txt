INTERNAL QUALITY ASSURANCE CELL
An Internal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC) is a vital component of higher education institutions, established to ensure and
enhance the quality of education and overall institutional performance. It functions as a self-regulatory body, promoting a
culture of continuous improvement and accountability. Quality sustenance and enhancement in education are key
challenges in higher education. To institutionalize the process of continuous improvement, the Internal Quality Assurance
Cell (IQAC) was established at PCTE on 10 th July 2021.

PCTE RESEARCH AND DEVELOPMENT CELL
The Research and Development Cell’s mission is to promote research activities across various disciplines and provide a
supportive environment for faculty and students to engage in creative and impactful research.
Major Activities of the R & D Cell are Focused on:
1. Hosting national and international conferences, seminars, and workshops
2. Encouraging collaboration between different departments and academic disciplines to foster innovative research
solutions and address complex challenges.
3. Assisting faculty and students in preparing and submitting research papers, articles, and book chapters to reputable
journals and publishers, enhancing their visibility in the academic community.
4. Promoting the development and commercialisation of innovative
technologies and research outcomes through partnerships with
industries, stakeholders and with help of E-cell at PCTE.
5. Organising training sessions and capacity-building programs to
enhance research skills, methodologies, and knowledge of the latest
trends in various fields of study.
6. Offering mentorship and support for students in their research
projects, thesis, and dissertation work, guiding them through the
research process and requirements.
7. Identifying and facilitating access to funding sources for research
projects, including internal grants, government initiatives, and industry
partnerships.
