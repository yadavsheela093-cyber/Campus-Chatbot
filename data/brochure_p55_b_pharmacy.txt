PCTE Ludhiana official brochure 2026 - B.Pharmacy
B-Pharmacy
Duration: 4 Years
Total Seats: 100
Admission Criteria: Minimum 50% marks for general category & 45% marks for other categories.
Eligibility: Candidates with 10+2 examination (science academic stream) with Physics, Chemistry and Biology or
Mathematics or Any other qualification approved by the Pharmacy Council of India as equivalent to the above examination.
Provide that there shall be reservation of seats for the Scheduled Castes and the scheduled Tribes candidates in accordance
with the instructions issued by the Central Government /State Governments / Union territory administrations as the case
may be from time to time.
B. Pharmacy is an Undergraduate Degree Course in the pharma field. This course is affiliated by the IKGPTU &
approved by Pharmacy Council of India (PCI).
Beyond the Classroom
Herbal Hub: Students prepare herbs-based eatables
and put-up stalls and sell their products. This activity
enhances the marketing skills of students.
Collaborations: An MOU is signed with Velite
Pharmaceuticals, Solar Pharmaceuticals, Global heart
& Superspeciality Hospital, Mohandai Oswal &
Diagnostic Centers.
Medical Camp: Regular medical camps are organized
whereby free health check-ups and medicines are
offered to the community.
Seminars/Conferences: There are several seminars /
conferences which prepare the students for advanced
research in the pharmaceutical field.
Research: Faculty and students have published many
papers in national and international journals.
Placements: Our students have been able to secure
placements at Sun Pharma with a package of 4.8 Lacs
and Life Cell with a package of 4.2 Lacs. Other than
this, Cipla, Abbott, Fortis Hospital, DMC Hospital, SPS
Hospital are also few of the organizations where
pharmacy students are placed.
