PCTE/ICMR RESEARCH 2025
Organizing conferences within an institute plays a pivotal role in
fostering intellectual growth, knowledge exchange, and
professional development among its members. PCTE hosted its
13th International Multidisciplinary Conference on ‘The Confluence
of Fields for a Resilient World,’ marking a significant milestone in its
commitment to fostering global dialogue and academic excellence.
The conference served as a platform for scholars, researchers, and
industry experts from around the world to converge and exchange
ideas on a wide range of topics spanning various disciplines.
Through keynote speeches, panel discussions, and paper
presentations, participants had the opportunity to explore cuttingedge research, share best practices, and address pressing
challenges facing their respective fields.
VYAPAAR 3.0 & VIPANAN 2.0
Vyapaar 3.0 : Vyapaar is the annual business fest organized
by the Department of Business Management, PCTE Group of
Institutes, bringing together students from reputed
management and commerce colleges across Punjab. The fest
offers a competitive platform to address real-world business
challenges through events in marketing, strategy, finance, and
accounting, including Corporate Roadies, Business Quiz,
Marketing Mayhem, Financial Crunchers, and Business
Crossword. Participants compete for cash prizes and the
overall trophy, gaining practical exposure and industry
interaction.
Vipanan 2.0 : Vipanan is a marketing-focused academic event
conducted as part of Vyapaar, designed to translate classroom
learning into experiential exposure. The event engages
students in product conceptualization, branding, promotion, and sales strategy formulation, enhancing their
analytical, creative, communication, and teamwork skills, supported by structured evaluation and expert feedback
aligned with industry expectations.
ATHENA & HACKATHON 2025
ATHENA & HACKATHON is an annual inter-college IT fest
organized by the Department of Computer Science at PCTE
Group of Institutes. Participants from renowned engineering
colleges, polytechnic colleges, professional colleges, and
universities across Punjab take part in events such as
Hackathon, Graphic Designing, IT Quiz, Web Designing,
LAN Gaming, Blind Coding, Bridge Making, Photography,
and Videography.
