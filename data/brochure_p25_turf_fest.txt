Turf
Turf, our flagship inter-school cultural and literary
competition, sets the stage on fire every year. With close
to 45 schools vying for glory, it's a spirited battleground
of talent and creativity. The best institutions from
Ludhiana and neighboring satellite cities lock horns in a
fierce contest, all aiming to claim the coveted title and
cash prizes exceeding Rs. 2,50,000.
