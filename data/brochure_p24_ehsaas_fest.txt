Ehsaas
Since its inception in 1999, Ehsaas has stood as a symbol
of tradition and artistic excellence on our campus. More
than just an event, Ehsaas is a vibrant festival that
embraces and celebrates culture, creativity, and talent in
their most spectacular forms.
With cash prizes totalling 21,12,500 and the highly
sought-after Overall Trophy, the stakes are high. Ehsaas
draws 35-40 teams from the best colleges across Punjab,
all eager to showcase their skills in a wide array of
competitions.
