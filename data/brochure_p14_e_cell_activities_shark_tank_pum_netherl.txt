Interactive Session with Montek Singh
Ahluwalia Former Deputy Chairman,
Planning Commission of India
PCTE organized an exclusive session for MBA students with
Mr. Montek Singh Ahluwalia, one of India’s most renowned
economists. The event offered a valuable platform for
students to engage on key economic issues such as inflation,
growth, reforms, GST, NITI Aayog, and the vision of ‘Viksit
Bharat’.
Mr. Ahluwalia shared insightful perspectives on fiscal
management, national debt, subsidies, and India’s
development roadmap, emphasizing how political priorities
often shape economic decisions. Ms. Harpreet K. Kang, Dean
of International Affairs, raised thoughtful points on Viksit
Bharat, enriching the discussion further.
The interactive session inspired students to think critically
about India’s evolving economy and their role in driving
future growth.
