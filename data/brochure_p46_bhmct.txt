Bachelor of Hotel Management and Catering Technology (BHMCT)
Duration: 4 Years
Total Seats: 180
Eligibility: 10+2 (any stream), Min. 50% marks for general category & 45% marks for other categories.
Hotel Management is a Degree Course that is approved by the All-India Council for Technical Education (AICTE).
The building of the department is built like a Mock Hotel, where one enters a lobby that leads to the Front Desk and the
Concierge's desk with a waiting area and Manager's Desk, and then it flows into a Banquet. Up stairs there is a Hotel Room,
Laundry Room, Bakery and Confectionery and a fully functional Restaurant named Indian Angeethi, Cubs Café offering
hands-on industry exposure through student-managed operations
Beyond the Classroom
Dignitaries Dine: Students cook and serve Indian, Continental,
European, American, Pan Asian, etc. to the High Commissioners, ViceChancellors, and Severe Industrialists. Additionally, PCTEians are trained
in Research & Development for the current hospitality trends to help
them in innovating their work.
Hospitality Olympics: An Inter College competition featuring young
Master Chef, Cocktail/Mocktail Making, Bed Making, Napkin Fold,
Service Relay Race, etc are organized by the students of Hotel
Management.
Annual Hospitality Conclave and conference: Famous personalities
from the hospitality industry and academicians participate every year to
widen the knowledge base of the students. Celebrity Chefs like Dr.
Sudhir Andrew, Chef Mandeep Singh Gill, Chef Sabhyasachi, Chef Sudhir
Sibbal and Chef Harpal Singh Sokhi have visited the campus so far.
Industrial Training: Students are given 6 months international as well
as domestic industrial training opportunities particularly in their 5th
Semester of the course. Internationally, this training is provided in
countries such as Mauritius, Dubai, Malaysia and the USA,Greece,France.
Domestically, the tie ups have mainly been set with Oberoi, Taj Hotels,
Marriott and many more. So far, the students of Hotel Management have
catered to events of CII, LMA (Ludhiana Management Association), Bonn
Breads, Trident, Cremica & Delmonte. Students are opting for successful
career options as an entrepreneur within different Restaurants and Cafe
Chains fields.

Placements: Our graduates have achieved remarkable placements
across the globe, making strides in renowned countries such as the USA,
Singapore, Canada, Australia, UAE, Kuwait, and more. They have
seamlessly integrated into the prestigious domains of 5-star deluxe
hotels, restaurant chains, and airlines, both in India and abroad,
contributing to the success of esteemed international brands. Our
alumni have become integral parts of iconic establishments such as
Omni Hotels, Burj-Al-Arab, J.W. Marriott Gaylord Opryland, Vistara,
Intercontinental, Shangri-La, Taj Group, Oberoi Group, Marriott,
McDonald’s, PizzaExpress, Chili’s, Ministry of Crab and more at different
hospitality positions such as Chef De Patie, Assistant Manager (AM),
Management Trainee (MT) and many more. Notably, 23 of our students
have achieved a milestone by securing the highest package of Rs 70 Lacs
in various states across the USA, showcasing the global recognition and
opportunities our institution provides.
