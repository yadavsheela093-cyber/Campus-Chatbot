PCTE Ludhiana official brochure 2026 - B.Tech CSE
B.Tech. Computer Science Engineering (CSE)
Duration: 4 Years
Total Seats : 210
Eligibility: 10+2 with Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects. For the remaining single course, the Candidate should have
passed any one subject out of: Chemistry/ Computer Science/Electronics/Information Technology/ Biology/ Informatics Practices/
Biotechnology/ Technical Vocational subject/ Agriculture/ Engineering Graphics/ Business Studies/ Entrepreneurship.
Department of computer Science and Engineering has positioned itself as a robust unit to facilitate learning and skill enhancement, to
meet the industry needs in specializations like Big Data Analytics, Cloud & Cyber security Services, IoT, Artificial Intelligence and many
other Digital Technologies required for Industry 4.0.
Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning play a key role in replacing human with machines by the virtue of solving complex problems of
the Industries. The Industries using Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML) include Agriculture, Education, Infrastructure,
Healthcare, Transport, Banking, Cyber Security, Manufacturing, Entertainment, Hospitality, and many more.
Core Specializations: Data science, Software design & development, Multimedia & Computer Vision, Information & Cyber Security.
Beyond the Classroom
Crewsphere & ICP: ICP India Hub aims to create an environment
where Developers, Start-Ups, and Blockchain enthusiasts can build
and connect. One of the key goals of ICP is to bridge the gap
between Web 2.0 to Web 3.0 by bringing in more developers,
entrepreneurs and investors. Crewsphere web3 team guided the
students in development of Projects like Tokenized Asset
Management, Blockchain based Identity verification, Decentralised
Voting System, Supply chain transparency etc.
Hands on Training: In-house faculties conduct hands on Training
on Automobile, Refrigeration & Air Conditioning and CATIA
Software.
Collaboration: PCTE Group of Institutes has signed 9 MoUs with
leading companies, including Novem Controls, Solitaire Infosys,
and Cresphere ICP India. Additionally, over 100 companies have
partnered with us for placements and internships, offering
extensive opportunities for our students.
Student's Forum: "Yantriki", is a platform provided to the students
to showcase their knowledge and talents.
Live Projects: The students work on live projects and also go on
Internships to enhance their learning. The final year students built
an innovative ‘Automated Conveyor Belt’ all by themselves and also
developed a ‘Curved Solar Air Heater’ that promotes eco-friendly
heating and reduces carbon emissions.
Job Prospects: The degree holders can work in the automobile,
chemical, aerospace, information technology, communication,
power generation and almost all types of product developing
industries. The engineers of this sector also work to maintain the
machineries of all industries. Mechanical engineers not only design
new mechanical systems but they are also responsible for testing,
maintaining and manufacturing them.
Industrial Visits: Industrial visits to several plants in the region like
SML Isuzu Ltd., RCF Kapurthala, International Tractors Ltd., G.S. Auto
International Ltd., Arora Irons & Steel Rolling Mills, Verka Milk Plant,
Amrit Soap Company, Central Tool Room, R&D Centre for Bicycles
and Sewing Machine and Arisudana Spinning Mills Ltd. and
organized. Beside this, students are exposed to techno-trade fairs
like MACH AUTO-EXPO.
