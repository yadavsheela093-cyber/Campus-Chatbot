B.A. Journalism & Mass Communication (BAJMC)
Duration: 3Years
Total Seats: 60
Eligibility: 10+2 (any stream)
Students in the Department of Journalism and Mass Communication at PCTE engage in a blend of practical training and
theoretical understanding, equipping them for success in the industry. They write articles, features, and news stories for an inhouse publications focused on live events. Additionally, students produce programs, campus diaries, and news bulletins for biweekly college radio, television, and YouTube channels.In our exclusive soundproof studio, students gain hands-on experience in
anchoring, voice-over techniques, editing, and design using user-friendly software. They also receive practical training in DSLR
cameras, drones, and gimbals while covering real-time news events both on campus and beyond.
Beyond the Classroom
Print Media: Students actively contribute to debates, letters to
the editor, and citizen journalist columns featured in various
newspapers. They also learn to use desktop publishing software
effectively.
Expert Sessions: We organize seminars, webinars, and
workshops featuring professionals from the media industry,
allowing students to interact with and learn from experienced
experts.
Broadcast Media: Involved in anchoring for radio and
television, students handle cameras, edit in-house television
segments, create short films, and write news stories in
broadcast formats. Their work is showcased on social media and
aired on Radio PCTE Ludhiana.
Industry Visits and Educational Trips: Students visit various
media organizations to gain practical insights into different
aspects of media communications. Additionally, internships are
an integral of the curriculum, where students gain experience
in recognized media houses.
Online Media: Students write pre-reporting, create social
media content, research based articles, and develop website
content, enhancing their skills in digital communication and
audience engagement.
Certificate Courses: Students are encouraged to pursue
certification courses on platforms like Swayam, NPTEL, Udemy,
Udacity, Canvas, Coursera, and edX.
Placements: Graduates secure positions with esteemed media
organizations such as News18, Zee Group, India News, Living
India, Times of India, Hindustan Times, The Tribune, Daily Post,
Dainik Bhaskar, and Jagran Group, PITAARA TV, Chaupal among
others.
