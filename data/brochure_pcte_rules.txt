PCTE Ludhiana - IMPORTANT RULES
Ragging is prohibited and students found guilty are expelled. If a student remains absent for 10 consecutive days in any faculty, the name is struck off and the seat is offered to the next deserving candidate. Fees once submitted will not be refunded. Jurisdiction of Ludhiana Court applies in case of disputes. AICTE rule: on withdrawal before the course starts, the fee is refunded after deduction of a processing fee of not more than Rs. 1000, and original certificates cannot be retained.
