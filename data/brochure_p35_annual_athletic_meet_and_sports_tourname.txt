Annual Athletic Meet & Sports Tournament
Annual Athletic Meet
The Annual Athletic Meet is an inter-class sports event
that begins with a colourful March Past by students,
who are dressed in their unique class attire and carry
their class flags. After the March Past, students
compete in various athletic events to win the overall
sports trophy. The meet also includes a Cross-Country
Race, in which students from different disciplines
participate to run for a cause.
Sports Tournaments
The college believes that physical fitness is essential for
students. PCTE has several sports clubs that, on a regular
basis throughout the year, organize leagues under the
name "All-Star League." In these leagues, different subclubs such as Falcon Fires, Thunder Wolves, Golden
Warriors, and XCEL Hoopers compete passionately and
enthusiastically against each other to win the
tournament.
