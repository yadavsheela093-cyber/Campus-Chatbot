PCTE Ludhiana official brochure 2026 - Hostel and guest house facilities (mess, food, rooms, wifi)
Hostels and the Guest house have these facilities: fully air-conditioned and well-furnished rooms; bed, refrigerator, mattress, curtains, cupboard and study table in the room; THREE MEALS PER DAY (food/mess is provided); 24/7 security guard; TV lounge and high-speed internet (Wi-Fi); parking space; recreational facilities like gym and sports; in-house laundry facility.
Girls hostel: near MBD Mall, five minutes drive from college. Boys hostel: in Jhande Village, at walking distance from Campus 2. A luxurious guest house is also available. Seats are allotted on a first-come, first-served basis.
Hostel monthly charges (w.e.f. 1 July 2026, incl. 5% GST): Guest House Single Rs. 7,900 / Double Rs. 7,400 / Triple Rs. 6,700. International Hostel Double Rs. 6,800 / Triple Rs. 6,200. Girls Hostel Single Rs. 7,300 / Double Rs. 6,800 / Triple Rs. 6,200. The brochure does not print a separate rate for the Boys hostel - ask the hostel/admission office.
