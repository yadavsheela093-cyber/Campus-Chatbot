Annual Convocation Ceremony
Convocation is a significant event that celebrates the academic
achievements of students, marking the culmination of their hard
work and dedication. This time-honored tradition serves as a
milestone in the lives of graduates, commemorating their
transition from students to accomplished professionals. At PCTE,
this grand ceremony reflects not only the academic excellence of its
students but also its commitment to fostering a diverse and
inclusive community.
This year, the convocation ceremony at PCTE was graced by the
esteemed presence Prof. (Dr.) Susheel Mittal, Vice Chancellor, IK
Gujral Punjab Technical University (IKGPTU), Jalandhar. His
inspiring words and distinguished presence enhanced the event,
making it a memorable occasion for all attendees.Col. P. R. Pandey
(Sena Medal) and Ms. Swati Goyal, Co-Chair, Women’s Forum,
Ludhiana Management Association (LMA), were present as
distinguished guests. Faculty, parents, and students attended in
large numbers to witness the occasion.
A highlight of the convocation was the vibrant parade that
celebrated the international diversity at PCTE. Students from
various countries proudly carried their national flags, leading the
ceremonial Academic Congregation. This unique tradition

symbolized unity and showcased the multicultural environment that

Each year, exceptional achievements are recognized with awards and
accolades, celebrating excellence across various domains. This year,
Ms. Swati Goyal, an alumnus from the MBA Batch of 2007- 2009, was
honored with the prestigious“Best Alumni Award” for her remarkable
career milestones. Among the current students, Ms. Shreya Bansal
from the BTech CSE Batch of 2021-2025 received the Wg. Cdr. H.S. Gill
Award for overall outstanding performance, and Mr. Trithesh Jain
from the Btech CSE Batch of 2022-2026 was recognized with the Best
Entrepreneurship Award.
Other notable achievements included recognitions in cultural and
athletic categories. Mr. Aneesh Mathur from Bcom (2024-2027) was
awarded in the literary category, and Mr. Mr. Damanpreet Singh
Sandhu from MBA (2024-2026) was honored in music. Additionally,
Mr. Pratham Bhatia from MBA (IB) was acknowledged for his
contributions to theatre, while Ms.Karine Ropi Jaseph Morgan from
BTech CE (2022-2026) shone in sports. These accolades underscored
PCTE’s dedication to holistic education and fostering talents across
diverse fields.
