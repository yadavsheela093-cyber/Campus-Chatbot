PCTE Ludhiana - ABOUT PCTE
PCTE (Punjab College of Technical Education), Ludhiana, was established in 1999 (ESTD. 1999) and completed 27+ years of educational excellence. It is approved by AICTE (Govt. of India) and the Pharmacy Council of India (PCI), and affiliated to IKG PTU (I.K. Gujral Punjab Technical University), Kapurthala. PCTE celebrated its 25th anniversary in 2024. It was founded by Wg. Cdr. HS Gill, Dr. K.N.S. Kang and Harpreet K. Kang. Dr. K.N.S. Kang is the Chairman and Director General of the PCTE Group of Institutes. Ms. Harpreet K. Kang is Dean of International Affairs.
