B.Sc. Fashion Designing (FD)
Duration: 3 Years
Total Seats: 60
Eligibility: 10+2 (any stream)
Fashion Designing program is a professionally structured undergraduate course to give students knowledge of the
industry. They start with the fundamentals of sketching to graphic designing where they are taught professional softwares
like corel draw, photoshop, adobe illustrator and learn about professional draping, garment construction, pattern making,
weaving, knitting, Traditional textiles , printing, dyeing process, Research based Design Process and Sustainable fashion .
The course encourages both traditional hands-on practices as well as the use of professional software.
