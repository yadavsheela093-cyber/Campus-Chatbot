PCTE Ludhiana official brochure 2026 - B.Tech Civil Engineering
B.Tech. Civil Engineering (CE)
Duration: 4 Years
Total Seats: 30
Eligibility: 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics as compulsory subjects.
Department of Civil Engineering at PCTE-IET is committed to produce professionally sound civil engineers of the future who are
well-versed in planning, conceiving, designing, building, monitoring, operating, and maintaining infrastructure, transportation,
and public utility projects. Our students develop an aptitude for research and a keen knowledge of various civil engineering
materials and learn to integrate them into developing infrastructure which effectively meets the users’ objectives and needs.
The department serves the society by imparting quality education with the motto of making the students knowledgeable and
employable. Since its inception, the department maintains consistently good academic record and produces quality civil
engineers.
Core Specializations: Structural Engineering & Construction Management, Environmental Engineering, Transportation (Roads,
Bridges, Railways, Airports, Docks, and Harbours)
Beyond the Classroom
Software Learning: Students enhance their skills by learning
professional software like AUTOCAD, BIM, GEOSTADIA, STAAD
PRO to analyze the Infrastructure of buildings, roads, highways,
dams, etc. as per the required scale.
Job prospects: The students of civil engineering deal with the
designing, development and maintenance of large infrastructure
projects. In addition, with consultancy services in this domain
also picking up, the professionals of the sector will find well-paid
opportunities in consulting across growing business verticals.
Civil Engineers can have a fulfilling career in fields like Real Estate,
Consulting and Infrastructure companies apart from the Indian
Engineering Services.
Live Projects: The students work on live projects & also go on
Survey camps to enhance their learning. Six month internship
builds a strong foundation in the domain of Civil Engineering
and instills analytical, technical, professional and management
skills in our students.
Industrial Visits: Industrial visits to several sites like Ambuja
Cement, Punjab Irrigation and Water Works Department,
National Fertilizers Ltd., Shahpur Kandi Dam project and Bhakra
Nangal Dam Project.
Facilities: Department Laboratories are fully reequipped with
high precision equipment and advanced software to enhance
learning in thrust areas of civil engineering.
Student Centered Learning: The students are exposed to liveindustrial and on-site projects. MoUs with leading construction
groups and real estate developers are signed to provide handson learning experience to the students.
Student's Forum: "Constructors" is a platform provided to the
students to showcase their knowledge and talent.
