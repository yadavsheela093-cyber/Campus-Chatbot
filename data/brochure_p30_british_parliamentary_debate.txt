British Parliamentary Debate
The Dr. D. R. Singh Memorial British Parliamentary
Debate was first pioneered in 2008 in India by the PCTE
Group of Institutes. It is an inter-college, two-day
literary fest, organized in collaboration with the
Ludhiana Management Association.
The competition attracts participants from across
India, with the 50 best teams from top institutes, such
as the IIMs, IITs, NITs, and others, competing in one of
the most prestigious and challenging debate
tournaments for cash prizes worth `1,50,000.

The competition aims to stimulate student's interest
through a diverse range of topics, helping them
achieve well-rounded development and improve
critical thinking.
Not only do the students engage in healthy debates,
but teams from leading colleges across the country
compete for more than 30 hours. Despite the grueling,
sleepless nights, they compete through knockout
debate rounds, facing a minimum of six debates until
the following afternoon.
