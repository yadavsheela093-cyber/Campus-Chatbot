PCTE Ludhiana official brochure 2026 - Chairman Dr. K.N.S. Kang message, vision and mission
Dr. K.N.S. Kang, Chairman and Director General of the PCTE
Group of Institutes, Ludhiana, is a renowned visionary in the
field of education. He has received prestigious awards,
including the 2015 Education Evangelist Award and the Best
Alumni Award, and is a prolific author and a driving force
beyond academia.
Dr. Kang began his academic journey with a degree in
Electrical Engineering, followed by an MBA and a Ph.D. in
Business Management. Notably, he ranked fourth in the
State Higher Secondary Examination.
He has edited six books, including notable works such as
Business Families of Ludhiana, which received personal
endorsement and high praise from former Indian President
Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam.
Another significant work, Business Groups in Punjab, holds a
special place in the exclusive library of the Prime Minister's
Office. In addition to his academic achievements, Dr. Kang is
a National-Level Athlete, a cultural champion, and a
recipient of the Shaheed Bhagat Singh State Youth Award.
