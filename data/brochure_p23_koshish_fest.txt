Koshish
Koshish, the most awaited annual celebration of arts,
culture, and community at PCTE, continued its
spectacular journey, bringing together a diverse crowd
of 70 enthusiastic teams competing in various events.
To date, the final day of Koshish has featured live
musical performances by renowned bands and artists,
including Havas Guruhi, Indian Ocean, Euphoria,
Jaspinder Narula, Nooran Sisters, Jasbir Jassi, Satinder
Sartaj, Harbhajan Mann, Gurnam Bhullar.
