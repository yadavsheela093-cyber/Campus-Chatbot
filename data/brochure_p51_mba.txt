Masters of Business Administration (MBA - Marketing, Finance & HR)
Duration: 2 Years
Total Seats: 120
Admission Criteria: GD/PI + Minimum 50% marks for general category & 45% marks for other categories.
Masters of Business Administration is a 2 Years Master's Degree approved by All-India Council for Technical Education (AICTE)
Beyond the Classroom
Sales Management: Students actively participate in real-world sales
activities, including merchandise sales and live projects. This hands-on
approach helps them understand consumer psychology, buying
behavior, and practical sales strategies while applying theoretical
concepts in real market conditions.
Business Research: Through applied research projects, students
investigate contemporary social and business issues. These projects
strengthen analytical thinking, data interpretation, and decisionmaking skills while providing real-time industry insights.
Business Simulation Games: Students engage in simulation-based
business environments where they make strategic decisions across
marketing, finance, operations, and HR. The focus is on value creation,
competitive strategy, and shareholder wealth maximization.
Partnership Hub: Students establish and manage partnership firms
by completing the full business setup process, including partnership
deed creation, GST registration, PAN application, UDYAM registration,
and bank account opening. They handle real transactions, gaining firsthand experience of legal, financial, and operational responsibilities.
Industry-Oriented Internships: MBA students undergo structured
internships with corporates and business organizations, gaining
exposure to managerial functions such as Marketing, Finance, HR,
Operations, and Analytics. These internships enable students to apply
classroom learning to real business challenges and develop workplace
readiness.
Grand Saturday Activity (Industry Immersion Program): The Grand
Saturday Activity is a unique industry-immersion initiative where MBA
students visit assigned companies every Saturday for extended
internships. This sustained exposure helps students understand
organizational culture, professional discipline, and operational
realities. With structured tasks, regular mentoring, and documented
learning outcomes, the program effec tively bridges the
academia–industry gap and provides clarity in career direction.
Case Study Analysis and Business News–Based Learning: Regular
case study discussions and analysis of current business news form an
integral part of the curriculum. This approach enhances critical
thinking, problem-solving abilities, and awareness of contemporary
business trends and global economic developments.
Certifications in NISM, Digital Marketing, and More: Students
pursue industry-recognized online certifications in areas such as
Digital Marketing, Financial Markets, Human Resources, and other
emerging domains, strengthening their professional credentials
beyond the core syllabus.
Money Multiplier Program: This initiative trains students to grow
enterprise wealth through innovative strategies, diversified business
models, and financial planning techniques, enhancing their strategic
and entrepreneurial mindset.
Workshops and Boot Camps: Students participate in interactive
workshops and boot camps focusing on professional grooming,
corporate etiquette, dining etiquette, global geography,
communication skills, and leadership development, preparing them
for corporate environments.
Placements: Our MBA graduates are placed with leading
organizations such as Goldman Sachs, Federal Bank, Trident, Jaro
Education, Tech Mahindra, Tata Consultancy Services, ICICI Bank, Asian
Paints, Crescendo Global, and Axis Bank. Students have secured
packages of up to 14.5 LPA at Federal Bank, reflecting the strong
industry relevance of the program.
