PCTE E-Cell Hatch Lab Incubator
PCTE E-Cell Hatch Lab Incubator, an integral initiative of the PCTE
Group of Institutes, is dedicated to cultivating innovation and
entrepreneurship with the core purpose of "Nation Building" —
creating employers, not employees, and driving the
entrepreneurial spirit of India. The PCTE E-Cell Hatch Lab
Incubator has developed a structured mentoring platform with a
proprietary framework to coach, mentor, and fund early-stage
startups, thereby increasing the survival rate in the first few years
of the startup and improving success rates.
Vision: To empower emerging entrepreneurs by creating an
environment that encourages ideation, innovation, and the
development of impactful businesses. We have the knowledge,
expertise, and capacity to scale the next generation of startups.
Mission: To facilitate the growth of startups by establishing a
supportive ecosystem, fostering industry connections, and
offering mentorship programs, with the ultimate goal of
contributing to the emergence of successful and sustainable
enterprises.
