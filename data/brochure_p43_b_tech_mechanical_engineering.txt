PCTE Ludhiana official brochure 2026 - B.Tech Mechanical Engineering
B.Tech. Mechanical Engineering (ME)
Duration: 4 Years
Total Seats: 30
Eligibility: 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics as compulsory subjects.
Mechanical Engineering involves the design, development, and manufacturing of products, systems, and processes in a
wide range of sectors like automation, aerospace, conventional and non-conventional power generation, robotics,
biomedical, and many more.
Department of Mechanical Engineering at PCTE-IET offers world-class theoretical education with hands-on-training in
various machinery, equipment, and software. Students get to work in laboratories such as Applied Thermodynamics, CAD/
CAM, Automation, Robotics & Mechatronics, Fluid Mechanics and Machinery, Heat Transfer, Manufacturing Technology,
Metallurgy & Heat Treatment among others, to hone the student into a fine mechanical engineer.
The students can opt for flourishing careers in the fields of manufacturing, automotive, maintenance, control &
instrumentation, and many more.
Core Specializations: AutoCAD and Simulation, Mechatronics & Automation, Energy Engineering, Automobile Engineering,
Manufacturing & Robotics.
Beyond the Classroom
Hands on Training: In-house faculties conduct hands on
Training on Automobile, Refrigeration & Air Conditioning and
CATIA Software.
Student's Forum: "Yantriki", is a platform provided to the
students to showcase their knowledge and talents.
Live Projects: The students work on live projects and also go
on Internships to enhance their learning. The final year
students built an innovative ‘Automated Conveyor Belt’ all by
themselves and also developed a ‘Curved Solar Air Heater’ that
promotes eco-friendly heating and reduces carbon emissions.
Job Prospects: The degree holders can work in the
automobile, chemical, aerospace, information technology,
communication, power generation and almost all types of
product developing industries. The engineers of this sector also
work to maintain the machineries of all industries. Mechanical
engineers not only design new mechanical systems but they are
also responsible for testing, maintaining and manufacturing
them.
Industrial Visits: Industrial visits to several plants in the region
like SML Isuzu Ltd., RCF Kapurthala, International Tractors Ltd.,
G.S. Auto International Ltd., Arora Irons & Steel Rolling Mills, Verka
Milk Plant, Amrit Soap Company, Central Tool Room, R&D Centre
for Bicycles and Sewing Machine and Arisudana Spinning Mills
Ltd. and organized. Beside this, students are exposed to technotrade fairs like MACH AUTO-EXPO.
