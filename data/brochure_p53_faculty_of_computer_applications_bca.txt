Faculty of Computer Applications
The Faculty of Computer Application offers Undergraduate and Postgraduate Courses which prepare the students for the
challenges of the digital world, they are provided with a dedicated Research Lab with state-of-the-art infrastructure where
they work on their researches and live projects. The students are exposed to technical knowledge related to Computer
Hardware and Software and explore various aspects related to web development, software development & designing,
Machine Learning, Python, loT, Android Development, etc.
