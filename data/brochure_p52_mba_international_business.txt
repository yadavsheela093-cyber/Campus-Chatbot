PCTE Ludhiana official brochure 2026 - MBA International Business
MBA in International Business (MBA-IB)
Duration: 2 Years
Total Seats: 60
Admission Criteria: GD/PI + Minimum 50% marks for general category & 45% marks for other categories.
MBA in International Business is a 2 Years Master's Degree approved by All-India Council for Technical Education (AICTE).
Beyond the Classroom
Barter Charter: It is a vigorous experiential exercise
that over night shar pens inter personal and
negotiation skills. Students learn to do business with
scarce resources. Strengthens their professional and
entrepreneurial skills.
Investment Hub: Students open a Demat Account
with a brokerage house and invest in stocks and IPOs.
Export Club: Students execute one real-time export
consignment during the tenure of the program.
50
Projects with Export Promotion Council: Live
projects are conducted with various Export Promotion
Councils like DGFT, ECGC, ITPO and Indian Institute of
Packaging, Mumbai. Workshop on International
Logistics: Students understand the multimode
transport system of exports by analyzing various trade
routes across the globe along with a visit to the dry
ports to have a better understanding of business
operations in logistics.
International Tie-ups: PCTE has active tie-ups with
24 International Business Schools from various parts of
the world. International Business Students participate
in various Exchange Programmes, International
Businesses, and Research opportunities.
International Marketing Projects: Students get
hands-on experience as they work on projects where
they identify new buyers, vendors for bulk supply,
prepare quotations for international buyers and select
the right logistic partners before shipment.
Vipnan : Marketing is the core of any business.
Students pitch their wits, talent, deduction, analytical
and creative skills for this rocking one day fast paced
annual event. Students learn to apply theoretical
concepts in marketing strategies. With industry and
corporate’s joining in to pitch and sell, or find a digital
clue from a data, it’s a pressure situation which only
the best can endure.
Placements: Goldman Sachs, Rex International, TRB
Exports Pvt. Ltd., Supple Tek Industries Pvt. Ltd., King
Exports, Eastman, Ralson, Centex Export are a few
companies where the students are placed.
