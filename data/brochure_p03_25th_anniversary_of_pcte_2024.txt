PCTE Celebrated 25th
Anniversary in 2024
PCTE proudly celebrated its 25th
anniversary,
commemorating
a
remarkable journey dedicated to
academic excellence, innovation, and
community engagement. Founded by
Wg. Cdr. HS Gill, Dr. K.N.S. Kang, and
Harpreet K. Kang, the campus radiates
pride in its transformation into a
distinguished educational hub, fostering
critical thinking and holistic student
development.
The day began with a solemn prayer
ceremony and the unveiling of the 25th
anniversary logo by Dr. K.N.S. Kang,
Chairman
and
Director
General.
Festivities followed, including a spirited
flash mob and a heartwarming human
formation of PCTE’s logo by students.
Dr. K.N.S. Kang expressed gratitude to
the students, faculty, alumni, and the
community, inspiring an entrepreneurial
spirit. PCTE pledges to uphold its legacy
of excellence for the next quartercentury and beyond, marking this
milestone
with
gratitude
and
anticipation for the future.
