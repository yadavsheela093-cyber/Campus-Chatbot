NATIONAL SERVICES SCHEME (NSS)
The National Service Scheme (NSS) is a public service program in India, aimed at developing students' personality
through community service. Established in 1969 by the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports, the NSS promotes a
sense of social responsibility, teamwork, and leadership among young people. Volunteers participate in a range of
activities, like Tree plantation, Cleaning campus , Health camps in villages, testing of drinking water in local area.
Woman health awareness program. from health and hygiene awareness to environmental conservation, literacy
drives, and disaster relief. Through these initiatives, the NSS fosters the spirit of national integration and community
development, encouraging students to contribute positively to society.
