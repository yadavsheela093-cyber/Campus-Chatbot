INNOVATION OF THE YEAR 2025-2026
PCTE Low-Cost FDM 3D Printer 2026
Final-year Mechanical Engineering students — Pap S. Nyanamah, Eba Elogne Tanoh Pierre Habib, Eitesh
Kumar, Joseph O. Carter, Karamoko Cheick Mohamed, Sahil Sharma, and Samantha Mahlanganise —
designed a low-cost FDM 3D printer to support affordable prototyping and academic experimentation. Using
Arduino Mega with RAMPS 1.4 and Marlin firmware, the open-source system allows full customization.
Equipped with a heated bed, stepper motors, and a high-temperature extruder, the project highlights skills in
mechanical design, electronics, and system integration.
Collaborative Robot (COBOT)
Training Program 2026
The Department of Mechanical Engineering
conducted an intensive 45-day in-house COBOT
training program for final-year students, led by
automation and robotics expert Er. Jagpal Singh.
Students received hands-on training in COBOT
programming, safety protocols, human–robot
interac tion, path planning, pick-and-place
operations, and sensor integration. The program also
focused on Industry 4.0 applications, including
assembly automation, inspection, packaging
assistance, and workflow optimization.
