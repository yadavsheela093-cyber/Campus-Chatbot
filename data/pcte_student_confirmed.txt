PCTE - extra facts confirmed by the college student admin
Timing of events (months only): Koshish, Ehsaas and Turf are usually held around October. The annual Sports Day / athletic meet is usually held around February-March.
Courses: B.A. Journalism and Mass Communication (BJMC / BAJMC) is offered at PCTE. BA LLB (law) is not offered at PCTE.
MCA (Master of Computer Applications) and B.Sc. Biotechnology are not in PCTE's current (March 2026) programme list and fee table; older website pages mentioned them. Please confirm with the admissions office on +91-161-2888500 before assuming they are available.
Library timings, the names of block-wise directors/heads (Engineering, Management, Pharmacy, Hotel Management) and the Boys hostel rate card are not available with the assistant. Please contact the college office on +91-161-2888500.
The PCTE website (www.pcte.edu.in) has an online "Apply Now" admission form, a "Pay Fee Online" page and a Scholarship Schemes page.
