PCTE Mr. & Miss Punjab
PCTE Mr. & Miss Punjab is a beauty pageant organized by PCTE to provide
the youth of the region with an opportunity to realize their dreams, be in
the limelight, fulfill their desires, and experience the world of fashion,
creativity, glamour, and style. Approximately 250 aspirants from the
region participate and have the chance to win cash prizes worth
`2,50,000.
The winners of PCTE Mr. & Miss Punjab received a cash prize of `75,000
each. Additionally, cash prizes of `25,000 each were awarded to the 1st
Runners-up, and `10,000 each to the 2nd Runners-up. In addition,
various titles were offered to the participants, such as Mr. & Miss Fitness,
Mr. & Miss Personality, and Mr. & Miss Top Model. The winners of Mr. & Miss
Fitness received a six-month membership from NYFC, Wave Mall. The
winners of Mr. & Miss Personality were awarded `5,000 each, while the
winners of Mr. & Miss Top Model received skincare vouchers from Kaya
Skin Clinic. The Mr. & Miss Influencer title winners received `5,000 each.
Raj Pandey (Sena Medal Commandant), Ms. Aditi Swaraj (Mrs. India
Globe 2016), and Ms. Mona Maan (Avid Traveller and Orator). Other
esteemed judges included Ms. Harpreet Kaur Kang (Dean, International
Affairs, PCTE) and Mr. Hassan Kang (Co-Founder, Vasda Punjab).
Sponsored by Jojo International and AU Small Finance Bank, with
support from Kaya Skin Care and NYFC Gym, the event attracted some
of the region’s brightest young talents. At PCTE, we believe in nurturing
talent, and PCTE Mr. & Miss Punjab provides a platform for Punjab’s
brightest stars to shine beyond boundaries.
