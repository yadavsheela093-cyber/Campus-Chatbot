Boarding & Lodging
We provide separate boarding and lodging facilities for Boys and Girls. The girls hostel is located
near MBD Mall which is five minutes drive from college and the boys hostel is located in Jhande
Village at a walking distance from Campus 2. Apart from two hostels, a luxuriously built guest house
is also available for the students. The allocation of seats is on the first come-first serve basis.
