INNOVATION OF THE YEAR 2025-2026
Military Research Project – From Concept to Prototype 2026
Our engineering students have successfully completed two defence-oriented projects in collaboration with the Indian
Army, involving the development of a fully functional electronic circuit design and infrastructure mapping software.
These projects reflect how PCTE students seamlessly integrate strong technical expertise with innovation and practical
problem-solving to deliver real-world, mission-ready solutions.
Building on this success, PCTE students and the Indian Army are currently collaborating on a third advanced software
project focused on inventory management, further strengthening our commitment to experiential learning, national
service, and industry-relevant education.
PCTE Drone Project 2025
PCTE demonstrates its strong focus on practical,
industry-oriented learning through a collaborative
quadcopter drone projec t. Students from
Mechanical Engineering, CSE, and BCA worked
together to design and build a functional drone,
gaining hands-on experience in structure design,
weight balancing, motor selec tion, flight
controllers, sensors, and safe flying practices.This
project provided real-world exposure to drone
technology and equipped students with essential
skills for careers in drones, robotics, and emerging
engineering fields.
